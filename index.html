Sola Corrado

Neuroscience & Computer Science Senior at Duke University

I am a senior undergraduate student at Duke University double majoring in neuroscience and computer science. I am particularly interested in the application of advancing technologies to analyze and model neuroscientific data to gain deeper insight into the mechanisms of the brain. As a member of the Imagination and Modal Cognition Lab at Duke, I am working on a project for my honors thesis on factors that influence the perceived plausibility of episodic counterfactual thoughts through functional connectivity analyses. My long-term goal is to continue leveraging computer science as a tool to analyze, restore, and augment neural functions and improve brain-computer interfaces to push the frontier in patient applications.
