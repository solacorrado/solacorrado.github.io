Sola Corrado

Neuroscience & Computer Science Senior at Duke University

I am a senior at Duke University double majoring in neuroscience and computer science. My interests are guided by a simple question: how do our brains work? I am particularly fascinated by the application of machine learning, computational modeling, and imaging analysis techniques to gain deeper insight into the mechanisms of the brain. As a member of the Imagination and Modal Cognition Lab at Duke, I am completing an honors thesis using functional connectivity to analyze factors that influence the perceived plausibility of episodic counterfactual thoughts. I have also conducted a computational neuroscience project at the Salk Institute modeling traveling wave activity in the cortex. My long-term goal is to continue leveraging computer science as a tool to analyze, restore, and augment neural functions, and to contribute to the development of brain-computer interfaces for patients.
