Sola Corrado

Neuroscience & Computer Science Senior at Duke University

I am a senior undergraduate student at Duke University double majoring in neuroscience and computer science. I am particularly interested in how advancing technologies can be used to analyze and model neuroscientific data to help us better understand the mechanisms of the brain. As a member of the Imagination and Modal Cognition Lab at Duke, I am working on a project for my senior thesis uncovering factors that influence the percieved plausibility of episodic counterfactual thoughts through functional connectivity analyses. My long term goals are to continue using computer science as a tool to develop brain-computer interfaces that can help patients.
